# Customer Deposits Analytics Dashboard

Power BI analytics solution developed to analyze customer deposit distribution and identify high-value banking segments using demographic and geographic insights.

---

## Business Problem

Retail banks manage large customer portfolios but often lack visibility into which customer segments contribute the most to total deposits.

This dashboard provides a clear analytical view of the deposit portfolio by customer demographics, occupation, and geographic distribution.

---

## Key Metrics

Total Customers: 3,994  
Total Deposit Balance: $236.93M  
Male Customers: 2,152  
Home Owners: 2,259  
Renters: 1,735  

---

## Analytical Scope

The dashboard analyzes deposit balances across multiple dimensions:

- Geographic distribution of deposits across US states
- Deposit segmentation by gender
- Deposit contribution by marital status
- Job classification based deposit comparison
- Education level analysis

---

## Key Insights

- Married customers contribute the largest share of deposits (~62% of total balance)
- White collar professionals represent the highest deposit segment
- Male customers account for approximately 54% of total deposits
- California shows the highest concentration of customer deposits
- Secondary education group contributes the largest share of deposits

---

## Dashboard Capabilities

- KPI monitoring for customer portfolio metrics
- Geographic deposit distribution visualization
- Customer demographic segmentation
- Interactive filtering by state and education
- Drill-through capability for detailed customer-level analysis

---

## Technologies Used

Power BI
